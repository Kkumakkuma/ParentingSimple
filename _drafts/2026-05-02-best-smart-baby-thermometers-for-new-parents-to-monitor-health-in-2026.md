Some parents believe that a thermometer's extensive features are always necessary, but often, simplicity is the key. I found that the straightforward design of the **Braun Thermoscan 7** made it easy to use compared to the overly complicated interfaces of some advanced models.  
**Why this matters:** Complicated features can lead to confusion during critical moments. As a new parent, having a direct and simple thermometer saved precious seconds when my baby needed it most.

### 3. Multi-Use for Older Children 

Some guides overlook the fact that many smart thermometers are versatile enough for older children too. For example, the **Withings Thermo**, while pricier, is an excellent choice as it can be used for toddlers and adults alike.  
**Why this matters:** Parents may end up buying multiple thermometers. By choosing one that grows with the family, you save money and reduce clutter.
