Why this matters: The assumption that higher-priced carriers come with better quality can lead to dissatisfaction. For example, I spent $200 on a popular brand only to regret the purchase because it lacked essential safety features, which cheaper options possessed.

### Claim: “All ergonomic carriers are the same.”
Why this matters: While many carriers claim to be ergonomic, they aren't equally effective. I found that the LILLEbaby Complete kept my baby in a natural position for longer periods than some more expensive models, crucial for both comfort and development.

### Claim: “Buying a budget carrier means compromising on safety.”
Why this matters: This isn't necessarily true, as I discovered with the Infantino Flip. It met safety standards while being budget-friendly, proving parents can find safe, effective carriers without overspending.
