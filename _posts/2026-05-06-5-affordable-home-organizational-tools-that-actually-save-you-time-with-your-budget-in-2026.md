## What Most Guides Get Wrong 

### Misconception 1: You Need Expensive Solutions 

Many guides tout pricey organizers as the only way to achieve success.  

**Why this matters:** Spending more doesn’t equate to superior results. I’ve found multiple affordable options that work just as well, if not better, as seen with my $10 Command Strips and $12 Sterilite bins.

### Misconception 2: Organization Happens Overnight 

It’s often assumed that a good organizing session will yield immediate results. 

**Why this matters:** I learned that organization is a gradual process. It took consistent effort over months to develop better habits and see results, especially regarding our children's understanding of where items belong.

### Misconception 3: DIY Solutions Are Always Cheaper 

Some organizers believe creating DIY solutions is the best route financially. 

**Why this matters:** DIY can be expensive if you factor in the time and materials. I evaluated time vs cost and learned that spending a little on the right products (like my $35 drawer organizer) saved me more frustration and ultimately time.
