## What Most Guides Get Wrong

### Claim: All Art Supplies Are Equally Engaging for Kids

Many articles suggest that any art supply is effective at igniting a child's creativity. Why this matters: My own experience shows that the type and quality of supplies directly affect engagement levels. For instance, my kids were much more inspired by the vibrant colors of Faber-Castell pencils versus a generic brand.

### Claim: Price Equates to Quality

Some parents believe that the pricier the art supply, the better it is. Why this matters: From my testing, I found that some affordable options, like Crayola’s Art Case, outperformed more expensive items like a premium watercolor set I tested that my kids ignored. 

### Claim: Every Child Will Love Painting

While painting is often hailed as a go-to art activity, it may not resonate with every child. Why this matters: I discovered that my son was more inclined to use colored pencils than watercolor paints. Opting for versatile supplies like the Sargent Art set can cater to various preferences, ensuring all kids can find their artistic niche.
